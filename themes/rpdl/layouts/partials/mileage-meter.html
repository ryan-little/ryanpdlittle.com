{{- /* mileage-meter — 2026 annual mileage progress, built from data/mileage.json. The JSON is generated by scripts/export-mileage.py, which reads Basecamp's database and exports aggregates only. This template does no math beyond formatting: the percentage, the remaining miles, the expected-pace mark and the pacing verdict all come from Basecamp, so the site can't disagree with the coach. The bar carries a second mark at `expected_pct`, where even pacing would put the year today. That reference is the point of the graphic: the fill alone says "64%", but fill against mark says "ahead", which is the thing worth knowing. The `updated` date renders as visible text on purpose. The number is a snapshot committed to the repo, not a live feed, so a stale meter has to look stale rather than look current.
