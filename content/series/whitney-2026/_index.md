The plan was always Whitney, and the Six-Pack of Peaks was the way to get there:
Wilson, Cucamonga, San Jacinto, Baldy, San Gorgonio, and San Bernardino, six
Southern California summits that climb from 5,710 feet to 11,499 and stack up
enough vertical to find out whether a 22-mile day at altitude is realistic. I
hiked them out of order, because a jammed finger from a kickball game pushed San
Bernardino back past two peaks it was supposed to come before, and I finished the
set on July 5th.

Langley came next, the first fourteener and the first time I could see Whitney
from a summit instead of on a map. Every number below comes off the Garmin, which
is why the distances here don't always match what the trail signs claim, and the
peaks are listed in the order I hiked them rather than the order the posts went
up, because those two drifted a couple of weeks apart almost immediately.
