# Mt Langley — Interview Questions

Answer these to write the post. Drop answers below each question. This slot was originally a blank "White Mountain Peak" stub for 7/24 — the real-world plan shifted back in early July (Langley became the 7/10-11 hike, White Mountain got deferred to August), so this is a fresh Q&A built off the actual hike.

**Basecamp stats (Garmin, "Mt Langley via New Army Pass and Cottonwood Lakes," 2026-07-11):** started 4:25am, 23.58 mi, 5,545 ft gain, 10h32m total (8h28m moving), avg HR 147, max HR 180, 4,562 cal, summit 14,044 ft, trailhead elevation ~9,992 ft, weather 63°F and fair, 34% humidity, light wind (4 mph).

**Confirmed via web search:** official Mt Langley elevation is 14,032 ft (Wikipedia, NAVD 88) — Garmin's 14,044 ft is a ~12 ft drift, negligible. It's the southernmost California fourteener. The trailhead is Horseshoe Meadow (~10,034 ft official, matches your ~9,992 ft GPS start almost exactly). New Army Pass is the longer-but-easier approach out of Cottonwood Lakes (vs. the steeper Old Army Pass); from the pass it's mostly cross-country/use-trail to a Class 1 summit push along cairns. Often called the easiest California 14er. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Langley), [Timberline Trails route notes](https://www.timberlinetrails.com/LangleyTrail.html).

## Questions to Answer

1. This is your first fourteener and by far your longest/highest day yet (23.6 mi, 5,545 ft gain, beats San Gorgonio's 18.4 mi on both counts). A 4:25am start suggests you were planning for a long day from the jump — was this always meant to be this big, or did the New Army Pass route end up longer than expected?

**A:** Always meant to be this big. Was hoping Old Army Pass might be passable (shorter), but there was still snow up there, so had to take New Army Pass instead.

2. Where does Langley fit in the Whitney prep plan? Is this "first Sierra 14er, proof of concept before Whitney," or something more specific — a permit-timing thing, a specific training goal for elevation/distance?

**A:** A training goal. Wanted to get a 14er under his belt to understand what he had coming up with Whitney.

3. Solo or with anyone else?

**A:** Solo, and solo car-camped the night before in the Horseshoe Meadow overflow parking lot (confirmed by IMG_0289, the chair/trailhead sunset shot at 7:07pm). Worked out well, except some people showed up around 11pm-midnight and were extremely loud, probably woke up the whole campground. He ended up summiting right as that group was leaving the peak, and beat them back to the car.

4. Your HR (avg 147, max 180) sits in the same range as Gorgonio (130/177) and Baldy (146/178) despite this being a much longer day at real altitude (14,000 ft vs ~11,500 ft for Gorgonio). How did the altitude actually feel — any symptoms, or was it a non-issue?

**A:** Definitely felt it above 12,000 ft. The last stretch, steep and sandy up to the peak, was really tough.

5. IMG_0334 (a waterfall on a granite slope) and IMG_0341 (golden-hour shot of Cottonwood Lakes) both look like early highlights before 6:15am. Worth building the opening around that stretch?

**A:** Started in the dark (a first for him) using a headlamp. The lakes were gorgeous, and the sunlight hitting Langley just as it began to hit the lakes was beautiful. The waterfall was unexpected but cool to walk by.

6. New Army Pass vs Old Army Pass — did you take New Army Pass both ways, or loop through Old Army Pass on the return?

**A:** New Army Pass both ways. Asked other hikers about Old Army Pass; consensus was it was likely passable going up but not safe coming down (snow).

7. The final push to the summit after the pass. What did that last stretch feel like?

**A:** Elevation was biting for sure by then — only 24 hours earlier he'd woken up at basically sea level. Pushed through with no real symptoms, but the steepness at the very end was punishing.

8. What was the view like from the top? Is Whitney actually visible in IMG_0399 (the summit panorama)?

**A:** Yes, Whitney is the tallest peak in that shot. Made him excited for what's coming, and it felt like Whitney was pretty possible for him.

9. 10h32m total with 8h28m moving means about 2 hours of stops. What accounted for the extra time?

**A:** Stopped for a long time at the peak, plus a few breathers here and there due to elevation. Proud of the 8.5 hours moving time for that distance/gain at that altitude. Booked it back down fast, took ibuprofen after descending New Army Pass, which helped a lot.

10. IMG_0435 (pack train of mules, wrangler riding lead, ~2:08pm). Random encounter, or headed somewhere specific?

**A:** Random trail encounter. The wrangler leading them worked for a campground up there — passed them on the trail. Seemed like a cool job.

11. Any other specific moment that stands out?

**A:** Not many other hikers out there overall, but some. Funny detail: got back to the car and realized he hadn't worn his Hoka Speedgoats like intended — accidentally brought and wore his Brooks Ghosts instead. They held up fine the whole day.

12. How are you feeling about Whitney now that Langley's done?

**A:** Excited and feeling prepared. Now has a real sense of what's in store. Also drove up to Whitney Portal the day before this hike (before heading to Horseshoe Meadow) to scope it out, so he's already seen the Whitney trailhead too.
