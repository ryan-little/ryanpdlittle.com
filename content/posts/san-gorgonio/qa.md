## Questions to Answer

1. One week after Baldy, back to back big weekends. How did your legs feel at the trailhead that morning, fresh or still carrying something from the week before?

2. This is the highest and longest of the Six-Pack (11,503 ft, 18.37 miles). Did it feel like "the big one" going in, or just the next hike on the list?

3. Vivian Creek Trail passes three established camps on the way up, Vivian Creek Camp, Halfway Camp, High Creek Camp. Did those function as mental checkpoints for you, or did they blur together?

4. 6:52am start, so before-dawn drive up to Forest Falls. What was that drive and the trailhead scene like?

5. Your average heart rate was 130, noticeably lower than Baldy's 146 despite this being a bigger effort by distance and gain. Was that a deliberate pacing choice, conserving for the longer day, or just how the effort distributed itself?

6. You were stopped for about 48 minutes total (7h57m total vs 7h9m moving), more than Baldy's 35. What accounted for that, longer summit stay, more breaks on the way up, something with the trail itself?

7. This is the highest point you've reached yet, higher than Jacinto's 10,834 and Baldy's 10,064. Did the altitude announce itself differently at 11,500 feet, or did the previous climbs prepare you for it?

8. What does the summit area itself look like? Broad and rocky, a single high point, something else? How does it compare to Jacinto's boulder-scramble top or Baldy's summit?

9. Visibility from the top, could you pick out other Six-Pack peaks (Jacinto, Baldy, San Bernardino if you knew where to look) or other landmarks?

10. Any specific moment that stands out the way the coyotes did on Jacinto or the crowd contrast did on Baldy? Wildlife, a person you talked to, a scare, a view that stopped you?

11. Weather and trail conditions late June, any exposed sun-heavy sections, any snow left this high up?

12. With Gorgonio done, how are you thinking about where you stand on the Whitney goal? This is the tallest thing you've climbed toward it so far, and Whitney is still roughly 3,000 feet higher.

13. Physical toll after two big weekends back to back. How did the days after compare to your recovery from Baldy or Jacinto?
