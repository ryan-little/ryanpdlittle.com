This one's short. The post that actually matters is next Friday, the Whitney write-up, and this is the housekeeping that has to happen first.

After Whitney goes up on the 28th, ryanpdlittle.com hibernates. No more posts here, and the domain eventually forwards to Substack. The trail writing already has a home: [Summit Register](https://summitregister.substack.com/), live now, nothing published on it yet.

The plan there is a weekly post through hiking season, working through the fourteen trail posts that ran here, rewritten and republished with new dates instead of backdated. Not in the order they happened, either. Three Trees goes first because tallest, oldest, and largest tree on Earth doesn't need three hundred words of who-I-am before it lands, and the Whitney arc gets held for later, once someone's actually invested in the person doing the climbing.

There's also a second one taking shape alongside it: [Pyrogeography](https://pyrogeography.substack.com/), wildfire investigations built off the actual incident reports instead of the press release version. Also nothing published yet, same as Summit Register, but the account's real and the name's decided.

What I haven't sorted out is where the build posts go, Primal Chase, Basecamp, whatever the RF viewshed work turns into. No answer yet.

Whitney's next.
