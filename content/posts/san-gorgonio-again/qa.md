normal trip report because the trail is already written up in the June post, so

**The finding, from splitting both FIT files at the high point.** The 43 minutes
did not come off the climb:

| | Jun 28 | Aug 2 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ascent (9.21 mi to the top) | 4:33:52 | 4:28:16 | **-5:36** |

| Descent | 2:58:57 | 2:33:21 | **-25:36** |

Same 9.21 miles to the high point both days. The ascent was 2% faster, basically
a wash. Nearly 60% of the whole improvement came off the descent, which is the
exact thing you complained about in the June post: "this hike really felt like
it took forever going from top to bottom," and you wondered whether to slow down
so your legs would hold up better going down. You went 25 minutes faster instead.

**What the coach logged:** "strong — hiked Gorgonio 43min faster than the 6/28
