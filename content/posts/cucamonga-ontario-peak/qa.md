# Cucamonga + Ontario Peak — Interview Questions

Answer these to write the post. Drop answers below each question.

## Questions to Answer

1. Why May 24th specifically? Was there something pulling you to do this one sooner, or did the date just open up?

2. Walk me through the morning before the trail. What time did you leave San Diego, what was the drive like, and what was the scene at Icehouse Canyon trailhead?

3. Icehouse Canyon is a specific kind of approach — wooded and canyon-walled, not open ridge. What did that section feel like early in the morning, and did the mist make it stranger or better?

4. The goal entry says "First sustained 8K+ ft altitude exposure." When did you actually feel the altitude change — specific point or gradual?

5. How did Cucamonga's summit compare to what you expected? What did you see from the top, how long did you spend up there?

6. Ontario Peak: planned or spontaneous? At what point did you decide to add it, and what went into that call given the mist, energy already spent, and blisters starting on the descent?

7. Describe the ridge traverse between Cucamonga and Ontario. What's the terrain like, how long does it take, what was the weather doing?

8. You had zero sleep going into this and hit 17.45 miles. When did the lack of sleep start to show up?

9. The Salvo 16 H2O pack was new on this trip. What went wrong with the shoulder straps?

10. The blood blisters on both heels — what was the descent terrain like, and was there a moment you knew something was going wrong with your feet?

11. Comparing to Mt Wilson: what felt physically different? Where did you feel the bigger elevation gain, higher summit, longer distance?

12. What did the drive home look like? How wrecked were you, what did you eat?

13. Ontario Peak is not in the Six-Pack. What made it worth doing that day, separate from the checkbox?

14. Were there other hikers on trail? What was the crowd situation on a Sunday in late May?

15. Two peaks in now. Standing at Ontario's summit or on the drive home — what's your read on the Whitney goal?

## Photo Ideas

- Canyon mouth at Icehouse Canyon trailhead in low morning light, creek and any old ruins visible through mist
- Looking up through the canyon walls showing the enclosed wooded character before the trail opens
- The snowline, wherever it appeared on the upper canyon heading toward the summit block
- Cucamonga summit marker or cairn with the mist behind it
- The ridge between Cucamonga and Ontario showing the traverse terrain and drop-offs
- Ontario Peak summit — self-timer shot emphasizing this one wasn't on the original plan
- Looking back at Cucamonga from the Ontario side with both summits in frame if visibility allowed
- Detail shot from the descent: heels, pack straps, boots — something showing the physical toll
