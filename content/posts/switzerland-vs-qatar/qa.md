# Qatar vs Switzerland: World Cup 2026 — Interview Questions

Answer these to write the post. Pre-match questions can be answered now; post-match after June 13.

## Pre-Match Questions

1. How did you actually end up with tickets? Walk through the process, timeline, cost.

2. Who are you going with, and what's their soccer background — following the tournament or going for the spectacle?

3. You grew up watching the Chargers leave, San Diego FC just launched. What does it mean to have a World Cup match in California?

4. Have you followed the Swiss national team at all, or is this more about being at a World Cup match than who's playing?

5. Qatar went 0-3 in 2022 as host, first host ever eliminated in the group stage, now they're back having actually qualified. Does that storyline do anything for you or is it noise?

6. Levi's Stadium is an NFL venue, not a soccer-specific ground. What are you expecting from the atmosphere?

7. Switzerland is a heavy favorite. Any part of you hoping Qatar pulls something off just for the chaos?

8. What's your honest read on your own soccer fandom — where does it sit relative to your other sports interests?

9. Noon Pacific on a Saturday. What's the plan for the day around the match?

10. Anything specific you want to walk away having seen, even if Switzerland wins 2-0 efficiently?

## Post-Match Questions
*(fill in after June 13)*

1. Describe the stadium when you walked in — capacity, crowd composition, where you were sitting, noise level before kickoff.

2. What was the split in the stands between Swiss and Qatari supporters? Any real tension or neutral crowd?

3. What happened in the match, and what moment will you actually remember a year from now?

4. Did the Granit Xhaka storyline land at all in person — 145-cap captain, fourth World Cup — or was he just a midfielder moving around?

5. The market consensus going in was Switzerland controlled, Under 2.5 goals. What actually happened?

6. Qatar has 25 domestic-league players on this squad. Could you feel the quality gap, or did atmosphere make it irrelevant?

7. Levi's Stadium versus what you expected — sound, crowd behavior, feel of soccer in an NFL building.

8. Biggest thing that went differently from expectations, good or bad?

9. Was there a stretch where the crowd got genuinely loud — where it stopped feeling like a daytime Saturday event?

10. You drove up from San Diego for this. Worth the trip in pure terms, as a soccer match to watch in person?

11. Would you go back for another group stage match if you had the chance, or hold out for a knockout round?
