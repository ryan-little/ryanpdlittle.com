## Questions to Answer

1. Three weeks passed between Jacinto (May 31) and this one (June 21). What filled that gap? Anything push the date, or was this just the next open weekend?

**A:** Had planned to hike San Bernardino Peak the first weekend of June, but broke his right ring finger (mallet finger) catching a kickball at a friend's birthday party the day before. Urgent care said to see a hand specialist, so he held off hiking until cleared. Also got busy with grad school work and took some deliberate time off training during the gap.

2. Where did you start from, Manker Flats? What was the trailhead scene like at 7am on a Sunday in June, this being one of the more popular SoCal hikes?

**A:** Started from Manker Flats. Lot of cars already there, but he got a solid parking spot close to the trailhead.

3. IMG_9932 at 8:35am looks like the Devil's Backbone section, narrow ridge, real exposure on both sides. What did that section actually feel like underfoot? Any point where it got your attention?

**A:** People online hype up Devil's Backbone, but having done Angels Landing earlier this year (the Zion trip), it was nothing to worry about. Felt comfortable, pretty standard mountain hiking honestly.

4. IMG_9919 shows you passing right by the ski lift towers and a stone building at 7:59am, has to be Baldy Notch. Did you consider taking the chairlift shortcut, and why skip it?

**A:** Didn't consider it at all, wanted the extra mileage for Whitney training. The lift was running that early, for what it's worth.

5. The title includes West Baldy as an add-on summit. Looking at the timestamps, IMG_9950 (9:33am) is your summit selfie at the main San Antonio/Baldy plaque, and then IMG_9964 and IMG_9966 (10:07-10:21am) look like they're from a different high point nearby, wide shots with what might be a second summit hump visible in IMG_9964. Is that second stop West Baldy? What's the connecting ridge between them like, and how much extra time/effort did it add?

**A:** Confirmed, it's West Baldy. Main Baldy summit had a ton of people, and the AllTrails route he was following actually continued all the way to West Baldy, so he decided to go over since it was easy enough, and he had West Baldy entirely to himself instead. Bonus: a better view of Iron Mountain and Mt. Baden-Powell from over there, both on his shortlist of future hikes. The connecting ridge between the two was easy; elevation gain is still a little rough but nothing bad after the main Baldy ascent.

6. Four Six-Pack peaks in now (or however you're counting Baldy in the sequence, correct me on the actual number). Did the altitude at 10,000+ feel any different this time compared to Jacinto, easier to manage or the same wall?

**A:** This was number 4, after Wilson, Cucamonga, and San Jacinto. Felt similar to San Jacinto, wasn't too bothered by it, though the steep final ascent to the summit still required some breaks.

7. Moving time was 4h49m but total was 5h24m, so about 35 minutes stopped somewhere. Summit time, photos, trail traffic on the narrow sections, something else?

**A:** All of the above, but mostly the summit break.

8. Crowds: Baldy is one of the most-hiked peaks in Southern California, and IMG_9914 and IMG_9942 both show other hikers on the trail ahead of you. What was the contrast like coming off Jacinto's two hours of total solitude?

**A:** Trail was busy, was around other hikers pretty constantly. On the way up he followed some people up a ski hill route instead of taking a side road, and regretted it once he realized on the way down that the ski hill route had been the harder way.

9. IMG_9973 at 11:26am shows a ski resort trail map mounted on a building, presumably back at the Notch on the descent. Anything happen on the way down worth mentioning, wildlife, gear issue, a wrong turn?

**A:** Descent was normal, just felt like it took forever to get down, which is a pretty standard feeling for him on descents for some reason.

10. IMG_9987 at 12:15pm, right near the end of your day, shows a tall waterfall with someone standing near the base for scale. Is that San Antonio Falls, the well-known spot near Manker Flats? Was this a planned detour on the way back to the car, and whose scale figure is that in the shot?

**A:** Confirmed, that's San Antonio Falls. He'd totally missed it on the way up, so he stopped for photos on the way down. There were people up at the top of the falls, and one guy (visible in the photo) had rappelled down the falls for some reason, not sure what that was about.

11. Weather that day, since late June can already be getting warm at lower elevations even if the summit stays cool? (Basecamp logged 59°F and haze.)

**A:** Weather was good, pretty clear, but LA was covered in haze/clouds lower down, which has been pretty standard on these hikes.

12. Where does this one rank for you against Cucamonga, Jacinto, and Wilson? Harder, easier, prettier, more crowded?

**A:** Ranks lowest of the four, mostly due to the number of people and the nature not being as good, likely because of the built-up ski resort infrastructure.

13. This was one week before you went and did San Gorgonio. Anything about how Baldy left your legs or your head heading into that turnaround?

**A:** Baldy felt fine, and he started exercising more regularly again afterward, though not to the same extent, knowing there were long, hard days still ahead.
