## Questions to Answer

1. Three weeks passed between Jacinto (May 31) and this one (June 21). What filled that gap? Anything push the date, or was this just the next open weekend?

2. Where did you start from, Manker Flats? What was the trailhead scene like at 7am on a Sunday in June, this being one of the more popular SoCal hikes?

3. Devil's Backbone has a reputation, narrow ridge with real exposure on both sides in parts. What did that section actually feel like underfoot? Any point where it got your attention?

4. Like Jacinto's tram, Baldy has a ski lift shortcut from Baldy Notch. Did you consider it, and why skip it again?

5. The title includes West Baldy as an add-on summit. What made you tack that on? Is it a spur off the ridge, and how much extra time/effort did it cost?

6. Four Six-Pack peaks in now (or however you're counting Baldy in the sequence, correct me on the actual number). Did the altitude at 10,000+ feel any different this time compared to Jacinto, easier to manage or the same wall?

7. Moving time was 4h49m but total was 5h24m, so about 35 minutes stopped somewhere. What were the breaks for? Summit time, photos, trail traffic on the narrow sections, something else?

8. Crowds: Baldy is one of the most-hiked peaks in Southern California. What was the contrast like coming off Jacinto's two hours of total solitude? More people at the summit, on the trail, both?

9. Any notable moment on the way down, wildlife, gear issue, a wrong turn, anything that stuck with you the way the coyotes or the overgrown trail did on Jacinto?

10. Weather that day, since late June can already be getting warm at lower elevations even if the summit stays cool?

11. Where does this one rank for you against Cucamonga, Jacinto, and Wilson? Harder, easier, prettier, more crowded?

12. This was one week before you went and did San Gorgonio. Anything about how Baldy left your legs or your head heading into that turnaround?
